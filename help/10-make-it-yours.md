# Making it yours

All of this is in [Preferences](#do=preferences).

- **Language:** English, Spanish, French or German, under General.
- **Themes and fonts:** under Appearance, with **Customize Theme…** for your own accent, panel and background colours.
  Text on your panels changes to stay readable. Panel and background colours stay in the app, so exports don't use them,
  and High Contrast keeps its own. With Glass on, panels stay solid (not see-through) over colours you've chosen, with the sheen of glass on top. The **Metallic** look works with your colours as they are, and its light and shade follow them.
- **The Look:** under Appearance, or the moon button. **Solid** is flat. **Metallic** gives the toolbar, tabs, sidebar, dialogs and the read-aloud bar a brushed-metal finish, with bevelled buttons and recessed text boxes. **Glass** frosts them, makes each toolbar button a small pane, and puts a soft glow around the search boxes. The page you read and the editor always stay flat, and so do printing and exports. High Contrast, a computer set to forced colours and one set for more contrast all keep the flat look.
- **Add-ons:** under Appearance. They add code-block types, export formats and themes.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** under Shortcuts, where you can change any of them.
- **Menu bar:** under General, choose whether it always shows.

This Help Center is in English for now.
